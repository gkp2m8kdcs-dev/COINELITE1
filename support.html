Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does COINELITE connect to a real brokerage?

No. COINELITE uses simulated (mock) market data for quotes, charts, and news. It is an analysis and tracking tool — it does not place real trades.

2. Where is my data stored?

All portfolios, watchlists, calculator inputs, posts, and photos are stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the App permanently removes all of that data.

3. Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no cloud sync.

4. Why did the App ask for Camera or Photos permission?

Those permissions are only used when you choose to attach a photo to a news post. You can decline and still use every other feature.

5. How do I delete my data?

Delete the App from your iPhone. Because nothing is stored on a server, this removes every portfolio, post, and preference.

6. What iOS version do I need?

iOS 16 or later, on iPhone.
