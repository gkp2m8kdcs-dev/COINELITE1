Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

COINELITE ("the App", "we", "us") is designed with privacy as a core principle. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles, how it is stored, and what we do — and do not do — with your information.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal account is needed to use the App.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or address.
  • We do not track your identity, device identifier, or advertising ID.
  • We do not record your portfolio, watchlist, notes, or trade history on any server.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or social tracking pixels.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your iPhone and never transmitted to us:

  • Portfolio holdings, average cost, and base currency
  • Watchlist symbols and order records
  • Calculator inputs and saved results
  • News posts, comments, and attached photos you create within the App
  • App preferences and display settings

You can remove this data at any time by deleting the App, which erases all locally stored content.

3. Photos You Attach to Posts

When you attach a photo to a news post, COINELITE accesses the photo only at the moment you choose it from your library or capture it with the camera. Photos are stored locally with the post and are not uploaded anywhere by the App.

4. Market Data

The App uses simulated (mock) market data for quotes, charts, and news. No request is made to a brokerage or live market feed on your behalf, and no transactional data leaves the device.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — used solely to capture a photo you choose to attach to a post.
  • Photo Library — used solely to select a photo you choose to attach to a post.

You may decline either permission and continue using all other features of the App.

6. Children

COINELITE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Because the App does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly.
