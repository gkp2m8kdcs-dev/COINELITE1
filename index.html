COINELITE

Global Markets · Smart Investing

COINELITE is a professional iOS investment app for tracking, analyzing, and managing global equity positions with institutional-grade tools — built exclusively for iPhone.

What You Can Do

  • Browse real-time quotes across 30+ leading stocks spanning every major sector
  • Study interactive candlestick charts with MA5 / MA20 moving averages and volume bars
  • Execute market or limit orders with a single tap
  • Build unlimited portfolios — each color-coded with its own base currency
  • Track live profit-and-loss, average cost basis, and sector allocation as prices move

Eight Purpose-Built Calculators

  • Compound Interest
  • Position Size
  • Risk / Reward
  • Dividend Yield
  • Currency Converter
  • Moving Average Crossover
  • Dollar-Cost Averaging
  • Inflation Impact

Turn complex scenarios into clear numbers, instantly.

Stay Connected to the Market

  • Curated news feed with comments
  • Share your own analysis with rich-text posts
  • Attach photos captured straight from the camera or your library

Privacy-First by Design

All data is stored locally on your device. No account, no cloud sync, no tracking. Your portfolios and notes never leave your iPhone unless you choose to share them.

Requirements

iPhone · iOS 16 and above.
